02 — OUR STORY
“Design can, and must, improve the spaces we live and work in — and, with them, our quality of life.”

The story of Italian Office General Trading Co. began in 1991 with the clear vision of its founder. That conviction still guides the company today — now one of Kuwait's leading names in office furniture and a member of the Eureka Group.

03 — WHAT GUIDES US
i.

Vision

A belief, held by our founder since 1991, that good design improves the spaces we live and work in — and that this improvement is worth pursuing without compromise.

ii.

Mission

To conceive and deliver interior solutions at every level, offering our clients a total design experience — backed by consolidated know-how and a global network of partners.

iii.

Experience

A journey through materials and artisan know-how, giving clients access to a broad range of products and customisation to furnish the spaces they inhabit.
